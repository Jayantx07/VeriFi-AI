# VeriFi AI launch video · plan

## Concept: "The Instruction"
One object carries the whole film: **a payment instruction slip** (₹5,00,00,000 → Meridian Advisory LLP · A/c ••••7812).
It never leaves the screen. Everything that happens in the story happens *to the slip*, the same way VeriFi works on the
instruction rather than on the call. Its status pill narrates the state: PENDING → RELEASING… → HELD → DENIED BY CFO → HELD FOR REVIEW.
In the final shot the slip shrinks into the VeriFi AI logo mark.

## Rules (from feedback on the first cut)
- Slow pace: one sentence on screen at a time, fixed caption position, key words in red, ≥ 0.35 s per word of settled hold.
- The visual illustrates only that sentence; props around the slip are minimal and fade in/out (the slip itself never cuts).
- Say plainly what it is: "It doesn't judge the call. It checks the instruction."
- Step tracker (01 Trace · 02 Confirm · 03 Respond) during the how-it-works section.
